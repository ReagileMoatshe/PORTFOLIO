Photometric Redshift Prediction

IndabaX Botswana: Food Price Forecasting

Forecast Botswana’s monthly food price inflation by merging five macroeconomic and price datasets. Led data merging and feature engineering, ran Granger causality and OLS analysis, and organised the notebooks and reports. The final LSTM was benchmarked against an XGBoost baseline.

LSTMXGBoostTime series
